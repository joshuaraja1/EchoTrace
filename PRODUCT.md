# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is commercial fleet safety leaders and incident investigators who receive
fragmented video, images, statements, and sensor evidence after a collision. They need to understand
the sequence quickly, verify every important conclusion, and preserve human judgment. The landing
page must also let Imagine Cup judges and potential pilot partners understand the differentiated
technical idea within one minute.

## Product Purpose

EchoTrace turns disconnected incident evidence into one synchronized, explainable reconstruction.
It aligns recordings, detects candidate objects and events, builds a unified timeline, and grounds
every important conclusion in inspectable source evidence. Success means a visitor understands that
EchoTrace is an evidence reconstruction engine, not a dashcam product or generic AI chatbot, and
feels confident opening an investigation.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, human. The voice is calm under pressure, technically credible, and careful
about uncertainty. It should feel like a trusted investigation instrument without becoming cold,
militaristic, or legally accusatory.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic gradient-heavy AI startup, a surveillance command center, dashcam fleet
management software, insurance claims automation, or a chatbot wrapper. Avoid floating feature-card
grids, vague claims, synthetic performance metrics, liability language, decorative glass panels,
and futuristic visuals that imply capabilities the product does not have.

## Design Principles

1. Show reconstruction instead of merely describing it.
2. Make the evidence chain visible at every important moment.
3. Explain the workflow in the language of an investigator, not an ML engineer.
4. Treat uncertainty and human review as trust features.
5. Move from fragmented inputs to one coherent outcome as the page narrative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Do not communicate confidence or status through
color alone. Honor reduced-motion preferences, preserve readable type at browser zoom, and keep the
core product explanation understandable without animation.
